About Robin

Hi, I’m Robin.

Occupational therapist for children and adults across the Waterberg — {{areasSentence}}.

therapy should feel human

photo of Robin

I studied psychology before I studied occupational therapy — and I’ve kept both ways of seeing people.

Psychology taught me to listen closely: to how people think and feel, what motivates them, and what quietly gets in the way. Occupational therapy gave me practical, hands-on ways to turn that understanding into change you can see in everyday life — a child who can finally write their name, or an adult who can make their own cup of tea again.

I’m bringing that to the Waterberg so families here don’t have to travel far for therapy. For now the practice comes to you — at home or at school — with rooms in Modimolle coming soon.

How I work

  1. Listen firstThe first session is unhurried. Your story, your routines and what matters to you shape everything that follows.
  2. Goals in your wordsWe agree on clear, practical goals together — things you’ll notice in daily life, not just on a test.
  3. Play is the workFor children, therapy is built around play, because that’s how children learn best.
  4. Parents, carers and teachers as partnersProgress happens between sessions too, so you’ll leave with simple things to practise.
  5. Honest about progressYou’ll always know where things stand — including when another professional could help, and who to see.
