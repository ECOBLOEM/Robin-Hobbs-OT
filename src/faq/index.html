Do I need a referral?

No — you can contact Robin directly. If a doctor, surgeon, teacher or another therapist has referred you, have their letter or report ready for the first session.

I’m a doctor, teacher or therapist. How do I refer?

Send a WhatsApp or call {{phone}} — with the family’s or patient’s permission — and include any relevant reports. With their consent, Robin will keep you updated on goals and progress.

Who do you see?

Children, and adults and older people. For children the focus is development, play, school and daily routines. For adults it’s physical and neurological rehabilitation — such as after a stroke, an injury or surgery. See services for more detail.

What happens in the first session?

An unhurried assessment. You talk through what’s happening and what you’d like to change, then Robin observes and uses standardised tests where they help. Together you agree on goals and a plan, so you leave knowing what comes next.

What should I bring or have ready?
  • Any referral letters, reports or test results (from doctors, teachers or other therapists)
  • For children: recent schoolwork or drawings, and the school report if you have it
  • For adults: a list of current medication, and any splints, braces or equipment you use
  • Glasses or hearing aids if you use them, and comfortable clothes

Fees & medical aid

Do you work with medical aids?

No. This is a cash practice: you pay the practice directly, and Robin doesn’t bill medical aids or submit claims on your behalf.

How much does therapy cost?

Rates are available on request — send a WhatsApp or call {{phone}} and Robin will explain the fees for an assessment, therapy sessions and visits in your area before you book.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or move a session, so the time can be offered to someone else. Late cancellations and missed sessions may be charged.

During therapy

How long does therapy take?

It depends on the goals. Some people need a few sessions and a home programme; others benefit from regular therapy over months. Progress is reviewed regularly and you’ll always know where things stand.

Can I join my child’s sessions?

Yes — parents are encouraged to join, especially at the start. Seeing the activities makes it much easier to practise at home.

Will there be “homework”?

Usually a short, practical home programme that fits into your normal day. It’s where a lot of progress happens.

Can you write reports for schools or doctors?

Yes, reports can be provided on request, with your written consent.

Your information

What happens to the details I send through the website?

They are used only to contact you about therapy. Please don’t include detailed medical information in the form. Read the privacy notice for how your information is stored and how to have it removed.
