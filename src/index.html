What is occupational therapy?

Your “occupations” are simply the things you do every day. OT helps you do them again — or for the first time.

We look at the whole person

Body, mind, routines and environment — not just the injury or the diagnosis.

We set goals that mean something

Buttoning a school shirt. Holding a pen. Making a cup of tea after a stroke. Getting back to work.

We build skills step by step

Play-based for children, practical and evidence-based for adults, with a plan to keep going at home.

About Robin

Hi, I’m Robin.

I’m an occupational therapist who believes therapy should feel human — warm, honest and focused on the things you actually want to do. I’m bringing that to the Waterberg so families here don’t have to travel far for care.

Whether it’s a toddler learning to hold a crayon or an adult relearning everyday tasks after a stroke, my job is the same: find what’s getting in the way, and build a clear path around it.

Do I need a referral?

No — you can contact Robin directly. If a doctor, surgeon or teacher has referred you, have any letters or reports ready for the first session.

Do you work with medical aids?

This is a cash practice: fees are paid directly and we don’t bill medical aids. Rates are available on request.

Where do sessions happen?

At your home, your child’s school or another agreed place in {{areasSentence}}. Rooms in Modimolle are coming soon.

What happens in the first session?

An unhurried assessment — we talk, observe and test, then agree on goals together. You leave knowing what the plan is.
